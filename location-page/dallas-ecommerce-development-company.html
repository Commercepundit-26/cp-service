Dallas Ecommerce Development FAQs

How much does ecommerce development cost in Dallas?
Budgets for Dallas ecommerce projects usually start around $15,000 for a standard Shopify build and can climb past $150,000 for a custom Adobe Commerce or headless project. What moves the number most is integration complexity (ERP, 3PL, PIM connections), how much custom design is involved, and how large your catalog is. You'll get an exact, fixed-price number after a free discovery call, not a range.
How long does it take to build an ecommerce website?
Most of our Dallas clients are live within 90 days of kickoff. Shopify Plus builds typically land in the 8 to 12 week range, while Adobe Commerce projects for manufacturers and distributors needing ERP or multi-site support usually take 16 to 24 weeks.
Do I need a local Dallas ecommerce developer?
Being local matters less than being available and reachable when you need us. Commerce Pundit works Central Time hours, so a Dallas-based team never waits overnight for a reply, and we're already fluent in Texas sales tax configuration and the DFW logistics networks most of our clients here depend on.
What ecommerce platform is best for my Dallas business?
Shopify Plus tends to be the right call for Dallas DTC brands in fashion and wellness who value speed and reliability. If you're a manufacturer, wholesale distributor, or B2B operation dealing with ERP connections or layered pricing rules, Adobe Commerce usually wins out instead. The recommendation follows your business model, we don't push one platform by default.
Can you migrate my existing store to a new platform?
Migration is one of our most requested services. We handle the product and customer data move, map every old URL to its new one so your rankings survive the switch, keep your order history intact, and cut over with zero downtime. We've handled migrations north of 10,000 SKUs off WooCommerce and legacy Magento installs.
